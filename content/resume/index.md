## Teaching, leadership, and competitions

**Prinicpal Instructor** at Stanford University
: *for [CS 40 Cloud Infrastructure and Scalable Application Deployment](https://infracourse.cloud), Jan 2024 -- Mar 2024*

* I [designed and taught](/blog/infracourse-how-to-setup) Stanford's first intro cloud computing course, enrolling 50 students in our Winter 2024 offering. I created ten weeks of new lecture content, designed several hands-on programming assignments making use of infrastructure as code, and built a lot of custom [infrastructure](/blog/infracourse-infra) to help support the course.
* We presented our work and our [lessons learned](/blog/infracourse-reflections) along the way at the [SIGCSE 2025 Technical Symposium](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.01032). 

**President, CCDC Linux & Cloud Lead, and CPTC Web Lead** at [Stanford Applied Cyber](https://applied-cyber.stanford.edu)
: *Stanford, CA, Jan 2021 -- Apr 2025*

* As President of Applied Cyber, I helped significantly grow our campus footprint by directing workshops and speaker events on web, mobile, and cloud security topics. I also helped increase the club's budget by 10x.
* I competed in CCDC for five years and CPTC for two years, leading Linux, cloud, and web security. We placed 1st (National Champions) at NCCDC 2023 and 2nd at CPTC9 Global Finals in 2024.
* I spent a lot of my spare time helping the Stanford entrepreneurial community with their security posture, disclosing a number of vulnerabilities to student startups. Sometimes this involved defending myself from [lawsuit threats](https://stanforddaily.com/2022/11/01/opinion-fizz-previously-compromised-its-users-privacy-it-may-do-so-again/).
* In 2023, I co-founded the [Stanford Security Clinic](https://securityclinic.org) to offer pro-bono security consultations to the Stanford community. We presented on our work and lessons learned at [BSidesSF 2025](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dtPopga8bdM).
