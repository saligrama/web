I'm a senior software engineer at [Formal](https://formal.ai) helping empower security teams to enforce least-privilege access for agents and humans using proxies. Previously, I worked on security and infrastructure at [Cloudflare](https://cloudflare.com) and [Verkada](https://verkada.com).

I graduated from Stanford University in 2024 with B.S. and M.S. degrees in computer science focusing on systems and security. At Stanford, I designed and taught a new [course](https://infracourse.cloud) on cloud infrastructure, led [Applied Cyber](https://applied-cyber.stanford.edu) competition teams to podium national [CCDC](https://nationalccdc.org/index.php/competition/about-ccdc/past-winners) and [CPTC](https://cp.tc/previous-winners) performances, and conducted security research with [Stanford ESRG](https://esrg.stanford.edu).
